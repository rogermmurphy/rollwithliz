Fuel cards: save up to 65¢ a gallon

Your biggest variable cost — cut down to size · 5 min read

Fuel is the single biggest cost you can actually control. The G Squared Fuel Card puts real money back in your pocket on every fill-up — up to 65¢ off a gallon, at 99% of stations nationwide.

💰 What 65¢ a gallon really adds up to

It doesn't sound like much… until you do the math on a working truck. Burn roughly 400 gallons a week and 65¢ off comes to about $260 a week — over $13,000 a year, per truck. That's not a rebate you wait for; it's margin you keep on every mile you were already going to run.

💡 Example only. Your savings depend on your miles, your MPG, and where you fuel — but for most carriers, the card pays for itself many times over.

🚀 How fuel advances work

Need cash for fuel before you've delivered? The advance process is simple:

  1. Secure your shipment.
  2. Submit your rate confirmation and pick-up Bill of Lading.
  3. Get approved funds promptly — so you can fuel up and roll.
  4. Request another advance on your next load as needed.
